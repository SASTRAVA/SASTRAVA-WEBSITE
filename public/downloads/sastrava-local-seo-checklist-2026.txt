SASTRAVA | Local SEO & Google Business Profile Checklist (2026)
https://sastrava.com

Use this checklist to plan a local search review. Search visibility and Google features can change; completing it does not guarantee rankings or leads.

BUSINESS INFORMATION
[ ] Use the same public business name, address, and phone on the website and business profiles.
[ ] Confirm that map links open the correct, owner-managed business listing.
[ ] Keep service areas accurate; do not create near-duplicate city pages.
[ ] Add real opening hours and contact options where customers can verify them.

WEBSITE
[ ] Give each important service page a useful, distinct title, description, and main heading.
[ ] Explain who the service helps, its scope, process, and next step.
[ ] Link service pages to relevant guidance, tools, and contact information.
[ ] Keep pages mobile-friendly, secure, accessible, and quick to use.
[ ] Maintain an XML sitemap and review crawl/index status in Search Console.

CONTENT & ANSWERS
[ ] Research customer questions and search intent before writing.
[ ] Provide clear answers, local context, and evidence for factual claims.
[ ] Include author or organization details where they help readers evaluate the page.
[ ] Review stale details and update content when facts change.

MEASUREMENT
[ ] Define a qualified inquiry and how it will be counted.
[ ] Track calls, forms, and messaging only with an appropriate privacy choice.
[ ] Review Search Console queries, landing pages, and indexing issues regularly.
[ ] Compare lead quality and cost before increasing paid-media spend.
